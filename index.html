About

Hello! I'm Najiya Naj, PhD Scholar at the Department of Computer Science & Engineering, Indraprastha Institute of Information Technology Delhi (IIIT-Delhi). Under the mentorship of Dr. Arani Bhattacharya at the WISE Lab.

My research focuses on remote vehicle operation and improving perception in adverse scenarios using different networks. My work is related to systems and networking, especially wireless communication and IoT for autonomous vehicles.
